During the final year of our Master’s degree, our team decided to take part in the university game jam one last time. This one was a complete sweep - we won every category.

We created ReWand, a game about fighting hordes of skeletons with the help of clones created from your previous runs. The project did not end with the game jam. We are now continuing its development together with a publisher who was also part of the jury.
