During the final year of our Master’s degree, our four-person team decided to take part in the university game jam one last time. We won it by a wide margin.

We created ReWand, a game about fighting hordes of skeletons with the help of clones created from your previous runs, a nod to the jam's theme of second chances. The project did not end with the game jam. We are now continuing its development together with a publisher who was also part of the jury.
