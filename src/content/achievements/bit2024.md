Knot Fun started here, built during the jam by a four-person team and taking first place on the theme "In a time loop". The name is a play on words: players are tied together by a rope, a loop of knots, and time only moves while you do, so standing still freezes it, literally a loop of time.

The jam version was designed for exactly three players. The whole Placeholders team later took the project over and expanded it to two to four players, and it went on to win a Render Cube sponsor award at ZTGK 2025.
