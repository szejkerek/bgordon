Lethal Speed is a first-person parkour game created by a six-person team. It was my first serious experience with building a 3D game in Unity, including humanoid animations, enemy AI, first-person combat, and a more advanced movement system.

The goal is simple: clear each level as quickly as possible by combining fast movement with close-range sword combat. Players can wall-run, slide, dash, climb ledges, use a grappling hook, and swing across the environment while trying to eliminate enemies without losing momentum.

I worked as the project manager and was also responsible for major gameplay systems, including player movement, enemy behaviour, weapons, and the general structure of the game.

The project taught me a lot about coordinating work in a larger team, connecting systems created by different people, and keeping a 3D game consistent across gameplay, animation, level design, and code.
