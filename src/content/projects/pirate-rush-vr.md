Pirate Rush VR is a rhythm-action game I created as my engineering thesis. It was designed for Oculus Quest 2 and can run directly on the headset without being connected to a PC.

The player takes the role of a pirate armed with a sabre and a flintlock pistol. Flying fruit can be sliced dynamically with the sword, while barrels must be destroyed with the pistol. The game also includes bombs, score multipliers, several difficulty levels, and a leaderboard for comparing results. A major part of the project was making the experience comfortable in VR. I added multiple movement and comfort settings aimed at reducing motion sickness, allowing players to adjust the game to their preferred way of playing.
