The Backyard is a small isometric resource-management game created during a 48-hour GameJamPlus event in Lublin by a five-person team. The game is set around a kid’s treehouse and was inspired by classic outdoor games and the general chaos of childhood backyard adventures.

The project was never released, and it did not win the competition, but it was a great team experience because several people were using Unity for the first time. A large part of the challenge was learning the engine, dividing responsibilities, and turning a rough idea into a playable prototype within a very limited timeframe.

One of the best ideas we developed was a modular character-customization system. Instead of drawing every character variation separately, our artist created a small set of interchangeable visual elements that could be combined into many different looks. It gave us much more variety without creating a huge amount of extra artwork.
