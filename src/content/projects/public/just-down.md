JustDown is a small 3D game demo built from scratch in C++ and OpenGL, without using a ready-made game engine. The idea was loosely inspired by *Only Up!*, but with the direction reversed—instead of climbing higher, the player keeps moving deeper underground.

The project became a practical playground for learning real-time graphics programming. It includes parallax mapping, multiple dynamic lights, HDR rendering, bloom, skyboxes, custom shaders, and a simple physics and collision system. Levels can also be edited through a text-based scene format without recompiling the game.

Building everything at a lower level taught me much more about how rendering pipelines, GPU buffers, shaders, framebuffers, lighting, and post-processing work behind the scenes. It was one of the most technically valuable projects I worked on and gave me a much stronger understanding of what game engines normally handle automatically.
