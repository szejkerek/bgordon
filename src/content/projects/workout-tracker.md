WorkoutTracker is a full-stack fitness web app created as a university group project by an eight-person team. The goal was to build a simple workout tracker with social features, combining gym progress tracking with a small community feed.

Users can create their own exercise library, log workouts, track body measurements, and view their training history in a calendar. The social side works like a lightweight fitness-focused Facebook, allowing users to follow each other, publish posts, leave comments, and react to progress updates.

The app was built with SvelteKit, TypeScript, and Firebase Firestore. Both the original concept and visual design were developed by the team, with each member responsible for a separate part of the product.
